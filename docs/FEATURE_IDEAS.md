# Rune Companion feature ideas

These features preserve Rune Companion's passive design: public web data, local calculations, and information the player deliberately enters. They do not require OCR, Accessibility Service control, or reading the OSRS client.

1. **Multi-account stat profiles** — save several display names and switch between progress dashboards.
2. **Daily and weekly XP charts** — keep timestamped public-hiscore snapshots and show skill gains over time.
3. **Skill goal planner** — choose a target level or XP, method, and deadline to calculate actions and XP per day.
4. **Banked-XP planner** — manually enter material quantities and calculate available XP, outputs, and missing supplies.
5. **GE price alerts** — notify when a watched item crosses a target buy/sell price or percentage move.
6. **GE portfolio and tax calculator** — record buys manually and track cost basis, sale value, tax, and profit.
7. **Detailed farming dashboard** — individual patch timers, compost state, payment notes, and configurable run routes.
8. **Slayer knowledge cards** — weakness, location, required items, cannon/barrage notes, and direct drop-table links.
9. **Boss and raid session log** — manual kills, deaths, duration, personal bests, supplies, and profit per hour.
10. **Collection and dry-streak dashboard** — target drops, rate, attempts, cumulative chance, and milestone alerts.
11. **Searchable clue companion** — manual clue-step search with STASH requirements, coordinates, and map links.
12. **Teleport route planner** — favourite fairy rings, spirit trees, jewellery teleports, and step-by-step routes.
13. **Manual combat and DPS comparison** — compare gear setups, attack styles, max hit, accuracy, and expected DPS.
14. **Consumable planner** — calculate food, prayer, runes, ammunition, charges, and expected trip duration.
15. **Combat Achievement planner** — manual completion checklist, points remaining, tiers, and recommended next tasks.
16. **Minigame reward calculators** — points, permits, reward rolls, XP, and time estimates for major minigames.
17. **Daily/weekly routine streaks** — recurring checklists for diaries, kingdom, tears, battlestaves, and other routines.
18. **Home-screen widgets** — next timer, tracked-player gains, GE watchlist, and best safe Shooting Star.
19. **Import/export and encrypted backup** — user-controlled JSON backup for timers, journals, profiles, and settings.
20. **Shareable loadout templates** — grouped gear/inventory quantities with notes and a compact image or text export.

The official 2026 mobile survey highlights demand for quest help, native notifications, clue help, and better bank tools. RuneLite's popular plugins similarly point to quest assistance, tile packs, banked XP, bank layouts, and activity helpers. Rune Companion should implement companion-safe versions that remain outside the live game client.
